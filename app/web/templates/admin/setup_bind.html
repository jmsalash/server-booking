Step 2 of 4 — Bind the group

  1. Add {% if bot_username %}@{{ bot_username }}{% else %}the bot{% endif %} to your lab's Telegram group as a normal member. Do not make it an admin, and leave privacy mode on (the BotFather default).
  2. In the machine-booking topic, send /bind (or /bind@{{ bot_username or "your_bot" }} if the group has other bots). Only group admins can do this. It also registers the topic for the topic list under Integrations.
  3. Your admin rights must not have “Remain anonymous” turned on, or the bot cannot tell who sent /bind.

The bot deletes its own confirmation after 30 seconds. Delete your /bind message by hand if you like: letting the bot do it would mean making it an admin with “Delete messages”, which also lets it read every message in the group.

The bot accepts the first /bind for 15 minutes after this page was opened.
